# Cabot Water Damage Help Lead Buyer Prospect Plan

## Opportunity

Urgent, high-ticket insurance-driven leads in a fast-growing suburb with emergency search intent.

## Search Queries Reviewed

- Cabot AR water damage restoration
- water mitigation Cabot AR

## Best Buyer Profile

- Local business serving Cabot and Lonoke County.
- Weak website or no city/service page coverage.
- Answers calls quickly.
- Wants water damage restoration, flood cleanup, burst pipe cleanup, crawl space drying leads.
- Will accept tracked calls/forms and give feedback on lead quality.

## Outreach Script

> Hi, is this the right person for new water damage restoration jobs? I built a Cabot-focused quote request website for water damage restoration, flood cleanup, burst pipe cleanup, crawl space drying. I am looking for one local company to send the leads to first. Would you be open to taking a few tracked leads as a trial and only paying if the quality makes sense?

## Trial Offer

- 7 days or first 5 qualified leads.
- Qualified lead: call over 60 seconds or form with name, phone, city, project type, and details.
- Start with pay-per-lead by job type, then move to exclusive monthly rental after proof.
